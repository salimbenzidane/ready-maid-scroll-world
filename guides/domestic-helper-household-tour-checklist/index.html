Why a Household Tour is Critical for Onboarding

A household tour introduces your helper to the home environment, reducing errors and building trust. It covers safety protocols, like handling cleaning supplies or child-related hazards, and clarifies routines. This proactive step minimizes risks and helps your helper feel prepared, aligning with Malaysian household standards for domestic work.

Key Areas to Cover During the Tour

Focus on high-use areas: the kitchen for appliance safety and food storage, living spaces for cleaning routines, bedrooms for personal care tasks, and bathrooms for hygiene practices. Include storage locations for linens, toiletries, and emergency items. Demonstrate daily tasks like meal prep or childcare handovers, using clear, simple instructions to avoid confusion.

Safety and Emergency Procedures to Highlight

Emphasize safety checks: proper use of electrical appliances, safe lifting techniques, and child-proofing areas. Cover emergency contacts, first-aid kit locations, and fire safety basics. Discuss household rules on visitor policies or off-limits areas. This ensures your helper can respond calmly to incidents, promoting a secure home environment.

Communication Tips for Effective Onboarding

Use visual aids like written checklists or diagrams to reinforce verbal instructions. Encourage questions and repeat key points daily in the first week. Schedule brief feedback sessions to address concerns promptly. This approach fosters open dialogue, helping your helper adapt faster and align with household expectations.
