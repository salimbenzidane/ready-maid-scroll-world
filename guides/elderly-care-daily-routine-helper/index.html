Elderly Care Daily Routine Checklist for a Domestic Helper

Use a simple written routine that follows the family’s instructions for personal care, meals, movement support, household tasks, rest periods and handover notes. Keep duties clear, report unusual changes promptly, and never change medical or medication instructions without the family or responsible healthcare professional.

Start with the family’s written routine

Begin each day from the household’s agreed instructions. Confirm the expected wake-up time, personal-care tasks, meals, movement support, rest periods and household duties so the helper does not need to guess what should happen next.

Keep care and household duties separate

Write the elderly person’s support tasks separately from general housework. This helps the helper complete important care-related duties first while still keeping cooking, cleaning, laundry and other household responsibilities organised.

Use simple notes for handover

Record completed tasks and any changes the family should know about, such as appetite, sleep, mood, mobility or a change from the normal routine. Notes should describe what was observed without making a medical diagnosis.

Escalate instead of improvising

If the elderly person appears unwell, has a fall, refuses an important routine, or needs help beyond the agreed instructions, contact the employer or responsible family member promptly. Do not independently change medicine, treatment or professional care instructions.

Review the routine with the household

Families should review the routine with the helper regularly, especially when duties or the elderly person’s needs change. A short review keeps expectations clear and reduces misunderstandings about priorities, timing and responsibilities.

Frequently asked questions

Should the helper change medication times if the routine changes?

No. Medication and treatment instructions should only be changed by the responsible family member or healthcare professional.

What should the helper record each day?

Keep brief notes on completed duties and meaningful changes from the normal routine that the family should know about.
